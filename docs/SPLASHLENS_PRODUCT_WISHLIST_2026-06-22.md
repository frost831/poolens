# SplashLens Product Wishlist - Industry Traction Pass

## North Star

Give the pool tech more time back: fewer calls to senior techs, fewer manual hunts, cleaner proof, faster customer explanations, and less after-hours paperwork.

## Best "No One Has Done That" Feature

### Route Brain: photo-to-fix service companion

The tech points the camera at an equipment pad and SplashLens builds a temporary equipment tree for that stop:

- Manufacturer and hardware guesses: heater, pump, automation, salt cell, light transformer, valves, robot, sanitizer.
- Symptom intake: "light trips GFCI", "heater says LO", "robot will not climb", "Omni offline".
- Suggested field checks ordered by fastest safe proof first.
- Parts/search terms only after confidence is high enough.
- Customer-safe explanation generated from the tech's notes.
- Saves the result to that pool's Service Proof Passport.

Why it is cool: it turns SplashLens from a lookup table into a field co-pilot that understands the whole equipment pad as a system, while staying conservative and asking the tech to verify every finding.

## High-Value Feature Queue

1. Equipment Tree Builder
   - Manufacturer -> hardware -> model -> symptoms -> codes -> fixes -> recommended next check.
   - Let techs save equipment per pool profile.
   - Add "unknown model" flow with photo and visible label capture.

2. Voice Notes With Auto-Clean Report
   - Microphone note capture while walking away from the stop.
   - Turns rough spoken notes into: internal tech note, customer summary, recommendations, and next-visit reminder.
   - Keep offline/manual fallback for privacy and weak signal.

3. Senior Tech Escalation Packet
   - One tap builds a shareable packet: photos, symptoms, code, model, readings, what was already checked.
   - Saves senior tech time and trains newer techs on better escalation habits.

4. Lighting / Automation Wizard
   - Dedicated quick flow for: no light, wrong color, GFCI trip, Omni/iAquaLink/IntelliCenter offline, valve stuck.
   - These are frustrating calls where a structured checklist saves real time.

5. Part Confidence Ladder
   - PartSnap result levels: visible marking, likely family, possible match, unknown.
   - Do not show buying links until enough evidence exists.
   - Ask for missing proof: label close-up, union size, cord length, voltage, model plate.

6. Training Session Mode
   - Instructor can make a mini lesson from a real fault: scenario, expected checks, safety warnings, answer key.
   - Useful for CPO schools, service company onboarding, and industry partners.

7. "What Changed Since Last Visit?"
   - Compare today vs last saved pool profile/report.
   - Surface likely reason for a callback: chemistry swing, filter pressure, salt output, recurring equipment note.

8. Callback Risk Flag
   - Based on incomplete proof, unsafe readings, unresolved fault, or unverified part match.
   - Helps techs decide what must be documented before leaving.

9. Manufacturer Learning Pages
   - For each hardware family: what it is, common symptoms, common codes, field checks, parts to verify, when to call a pro.
   - SEO/AEO friendly and matches the new tree structure.

10. Quick Quote Prep
   - Not a full CRM. Generates clean recommendation text and evidence list for the office/owner.
   - Keeps SplashLens focused on field speed, not billing.

## Product Positioning

SplashLens should keep saying: "Built to get the tech off the pad faster, with better proof and less guessing." The app should avoid sounding like a replacement for training, manuals, licensed electrical work, gas work, refrigerant work, or commercial compliance.
