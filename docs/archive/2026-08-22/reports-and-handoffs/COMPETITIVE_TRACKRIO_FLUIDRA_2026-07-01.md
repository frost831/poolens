# Competitive Note - Fluidra trackr iO - 2026-07-01

## Signal

Fluidra/Zodiac has a live app and web presence for `trackr iO`.

Verified public sources checked 2026-07-01:

- Website: `https://www.trackrio.com/`
- Google Play: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zodiac.trackrio`
- Apple App Store: `https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trackr-io/id6761037792`
- Fluidra acquisition context: `https://www.fluidra.com/press-room/press-release/fluidra-accelerates-digital-strategy-with-acquisition-of-pooltrackr/`

## What trackr iO Is Claiming

trackr iO is positioned as an all-in-one pool and spa business platform.

Publicly listed capabilities include:

- Scheduling and dispatch.
- Routes.
- Invoicing and billing.
- Payments.
- Water testing.
- Customer management.
- Customer communication.
- Marketing campaign management.
- Batch billing.
- Barcode scanner.
- Customer reports.
- Retail/store workflow.
- Fluidra connected-equipment/data ecosystem positioning.

Public pricing on the trackr iO site: `$65 per technician, per month`.

Store listings identify the developer as Zodiac Pool Systems, Inc. and frame the app as built by Fluidra.

## Competitive Read

This is a serious CRM/business-operations signal, not a small utility.

Fluidra's advantage:

- Brand trust.
- Manufacturer/data ecosystem.
- Connected-equipment strategy.
- CRM/business platform surface.
- Existing Pooltrackr acquisition foundation.
- Ability to bundle software with manufacturer/dealer/distributor channels.

Likely weakness or opening:

- Heavier business-management platform.
- Paid per-tech seat model.
- May be slower for solo techs who need quick pad-side answers.
- Likely optimized for company workflow, not mystery-part proof, cautious field identification, or senior-tech/vendor packet creation.
- Manufacturer-owned ecosystem may make non-Fluidra neutrality harder to message.

## SplashLens Positioning Response

Do not chase trackr iO head-on as a full CRM right now.

SplashLens should position as:

- The field intelligence layer before the CRM entry.
- The fastest pad-side reference, proof, and escalation workflow.
- The no-account/free reference aid a solo tech can open immediately.
- The tool that helps a tech capture better evidence before entering Jobber, Skimmer, Pool Brain, trackr iO, QuickBooks, or a vendor counter.

Suggested copy angle:

`Trackr iO runs the business. SplashLens helps the tech solve the stop.`

Use carefully. Do not directly attack or imply a formal comparison unless we publish a fair comparison page.
