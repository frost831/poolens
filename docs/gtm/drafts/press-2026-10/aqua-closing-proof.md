## Email body

Hello AQUA team,

Thank you for covering SplashLens Closing Season Mode in August. For a service-focused follow-up, I can offer a short, practical piece on what to document before a pool is closed: the starting water and equipment condition, the drain and plug work, the cover setup, and any customer decision to defer a repair. The story is less about an app launch than about leaving the spring crew a legible record.

The workflow asks a tech to capture chemistry and water level, equipment-pad photos, drain-plug proof, cover condition, and heater, salt, or robot notes. A useful sidebar could show how to label a photo, record what remains unverified, and write a customer-safe summary without implying that a checklist guarantees a freeze-proof close. Exact winterization steps still come from the equipment manual, local requirements, and the qualified tech on site.

Would a concise how-to with a staged, customer-free visual sequence be useful for AQUA's service readers?

Joshua Frost
