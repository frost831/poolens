# PoolPro follow-up: first fall, what techs miss at closing

**Status:** Draft only; no send.

**Subject:** PoolPro follow-up: the closing details techs should record before spring

## Email body

Hello PoolPro team,

Thank you for covering SplashLens at launch in June and Closing Season Mode in September. For an October service piece, I'd suggest a first-fall field checklist: what can be easy to leave out of a closing record when the last stop is rushed? The angle is practical, not a claim that we have measured technicians' mistakes.

Start with a wide equipment-pad photo and water readings, then record drain plugs and where removed parts were stored. Add cover condition, heater or salt-system notes, and a plain-language account of any work the customer declined. Those details give the spring crew a starting point and make the handoff more useful than an unlabeled camera roll. SplashLens can organize the record, but each winterization decision still belongs to the manual, local requirements, company procedure, and qualified tech.

We have no verified outcomes to publish from the ten-tech closing pilot. Would a short checklist article with staged screenshots, rather than a case study, fit your fall coverage?

Joshua Frost
