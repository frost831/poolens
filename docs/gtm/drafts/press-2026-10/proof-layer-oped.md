# Op-ed pitch: the proof layer beside connected pool data

**Status:** Draft only; no send.

**Subject:** Op-ed idea: connected pool alerts still need on-pad proof

## Email body

Hello editor,

Pentair and Pool Brain are connecting IntelliFlo3 pump data with service workflows. That is a useful advance: the office can see equipment signals before a visit. My proposed op-ed asks what happens after the alert, when a technician reaches the pad and must show what was visible, what was checked, and what remains uncertain.

An equipment event is not the same record as a model plate, a labeled photo, a reading, or a note about a customer decision. The two layers can complement each other: connected data helps frame the call; field evidence helps a colleague, supplier, or homeowner understand the next move. SplashLens is built for that second layer, with reference lookup and proof packets that can be copied into the service company's existing workflow.

This is an independent editorial argument, not a claim of integration or endorsement by Pentair or Pool Brain. Could I send a concise byline on how to make connected-service alerts actionable at the pad without replacing the CRM or the tech's judgment?

Joshua Frost

## Three verified facts

- Pool Brain announced in August 2026 that its Pentair IntelliFlo3 integration was live, with daily pump data and configurable alerts. [Pool Brain Help Center](https://help.poolbrain.com/en/articles/16056861-august-6-2026-new-update-released)
- Pentair describes IntelliFlo3 data in Pool Brain as including RPM, PSI, pump alerts, and Wi-Fi connection status. [Pentair integration page](https://www.flowandfiltrationsolutions.pentair.com/en-us/pool-spa/education-support/professional-support/pool-and-spa-education/if3-vsf-and-pool-brain.html)
- SplashLens describes its field workflow as capturing photos, readings, notes, missing checks, and handoff details; its app offers copy/download proof for use alongside a CRM. [SplashLens web app](https://app.splashlens.com/)
