# SplashLens Verified Proof Network

Date: 2026-07-17

## Positioning

SplashLens stays free where trust matters most: manual lookup, basic PartSnap, basic calculators, core Facility Assist, and field-safe troubleshooting prompts.

The paid business layer is SplashLens Verified Proof Network:

> Free for techs. Paid for teams, facilities, trainers, distributors, and manufacturers who want cleaner proof before callbacks, part orders, warranty calls, or escalation.

## Monetization Lanes

### Free Core

Target: individual techs, CPOs, facility operators, counter staff, and homeowners who need a practical reference.

Includes:
- Manual lookup
- Basic PartSnap
- Basic calculators
- Basic Facility Assist
- Conservative field prompts

Guardrail: keep this genuinely useful so SplashLens keeps goodwill and industry trust.

### Service Proof Pro

Target price: $9-$19/month solo tech.

Includes:
- Saved Service Proof Passports

- Customer-safe summaries

- Callback risk flags
- Export/share
- History

Status: pilot lane. Do not market as a live paid entitlement until billing and unlocks are fully wired.

Status: pilot lane.

### Facility / CPO Pilot

Target price: $49-$199/month per facility or training org.

Includes:
- Daily checks
- Contamination event workflow

- Escalation packets

Status: pilot lane and strongest follow-up from the Aquatic Council conversation.

### Verified Manufacturer Cards

Target price: $500-$2,500/month plus setup, depending on size and scope.

Includes:
- Field-safe model family cards
- Known failure points
- Required proof photos
- Warranty-safe wording
- Preferred escalation steps

Status: partner lane. Never mark a card verified unless a real partner supplies and approves the language in writing.

### Distributor / Counter Mode

Target price: pilot priced.

Includes:

- Second photo proof

Status: pilot lane.

### Training Partner Layer

Target price: licensing or revenue share.
