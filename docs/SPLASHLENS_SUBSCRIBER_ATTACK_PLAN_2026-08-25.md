# SplashLens Subscriber Attack Plan - 2026-08-25

## Current Read

SplashLens is no longer blocked by basic wiring.

- First-party app and site event capture: live.

## North Star

Get a pool or spa pro from attention to one useful field action in under 60 seconds.

The paid ask should appear after value:

1. A PartSnap result is useful.
2. A closing-season proof record is created.
3. A customer-safe summary or vendor/senior-tech packet is wanted.
4. A company owner wants to see what techs are searching, missing, saving, or escalating.

## Immediate Campaign

Use this CTA:

> Try SplashLens on one real closing, repair note, code, or equipment family and tell me where it falls short.

Primary tracked link:

`https://splashlens.com/campaign.html?utm_source=outreach&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=closing_season_owner_20260825&utm_content=[recipient_slug]`

Use the broad homepage only for brand traffic. Use campaign/paid-media/closing-season pages for serious sends.

## Segments To Prioritize

1. Pool service owners
   - Goal: Solo Proof or Teams interest.
   - Message: cleaner notes, fewer spring questions, less “what happened on that stop?”

2. Closing/opening-heavy Midwest and seasonal companies
   - Goal: closing-season proof workflow adoption.
   - Message: capture proof before freeze season.

3. CPO/facility/operator trainers
   - Goal: Facility Assist pilot and Learning OS feedback.
   - Message: simple operator workflow for people who manage pools but do not repair everything themselves.

4. Distributor/counter/support teams
   - Goal: proof packet workflow.
   - Message: fewer vague part requests and cleaner escalation before ordering.

5. Manufacturers and reps
   - Goal: verified field cards.
   - Message: give us preferred proof language and known field misses; SplashLens turns it into conservative field-safe cards.

## Offer Stack

- Free: lookup, basic PartSnap, calculators, notes, checklists, Facility Assist previews.
- $4.99/mo: PartSnap Pro for heavier scanner use.
- $19/mo target: Solo Proof for saved job history and customer-safe summaries.
- $99/mo target: Teams for company visibility, proof records, repeated misses, and training prompts.
- Partner: verified manufacturer cards, distributor/counter mode, training partner layer.

## My Opinion

Do not lead with “AI app.” Lead with “get off the pad faster” and “prove the stop before it becomes a callback.”

The highest-value paid wedge is not generic subscriptions. It is proof:

- save the job trail,
- create the customer-safe summary,
- build the senior-tech/vendor packet,
- show owners what techs keep running into.

PartSnap gets attention. Closing Season Mode gives urgency. Teams/Solo Proof gives a business reason to pay.
