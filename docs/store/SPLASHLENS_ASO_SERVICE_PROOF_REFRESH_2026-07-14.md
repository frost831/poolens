# SplashLens ASO Refresh - Service Proof OS - July 14, 2026

## Short Description

Proof-first pool and spa field app: PartSnap, Service Proof Passport, Facility Assist, codes, dosing, robots, automation, and voice notes.

## Positioning

SplashLens helps pool and spa techs move faster at the stop by identifying possible part paths, capturing proof, building customer-safe summaries, and saving service memory before the next visit.

The flagship story is Service Proof OS:

- PartSnap identifies the mystery part or equipment clue.
- Service Proof Passport captures readings, photos, voice notes, proof status, callback risk, and next-visit context.
- Facility Assist gives CPOs and non-repair operators a lighter workflow for daily checks, contamination events, basic equipment proof, and escalation packets.
- Connected Pool Network covers robots, automation, lights, salt, spa packs, heat pumps, covers, and chemical controllers.
