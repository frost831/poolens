## B. What they have we don't (ranked by threat / attractiveness)

1. **Company operating system gravity (Skimmer / Pool Brain / Paythepoolman / Pool Founder / PoolDial)** — billing, routes, autopay, customer portal, service emails. Buyers already pay $49–$250+/mo. **Threat:** they absorb “good enough” chem + photos and leave no budget/mindshare for another sub. **Attractiveness if complement:** export-first proof packets into their reports.
2. **Distributor buy-path with live inventory (Heritage Pool+, POOL360)** — barcode, exploded diagrams, order-ahead, AI product Q&A (Heritage Aug 2025). **Threat:** if PartSnap can’t hand off to “order this SKU at my branch,” conversion stays soft. **Opportunity:** be the *pre-login* ID layer that feeds their carts.
3. **Instrument-grade chem (LaMotte Spin Touch → WaterLink → Skimmer/PB)** — Bluetooth readings eliminate typing. SplashLens test-strip photo is triage-only (own FAQ).
4. **OEM connected data streams (Pentair ↔ Pool Brain, May 2026)** — daily pump telemetry, proactive alerts. Proves manufacturers will open data to *route platforms that drive install/service volume*, not necessarily to independent field apps. **[HYPOTHESIS]** Hayward/Fluidra will follow similar CRM partnerships.
5. **Admin AI that owners feel (Pool Founder George, Skimmer AI Phone $99/mo add-on, PoolDial AI receptionist)** — answers phones, moves jobs, fills visits. Different buyer (owner) than SplashLens wedge (tech on pad).
6. **Deep LSI brand (Orenda)** — free, culturally entrenched, embedded in Skimmer Scaling Up. Competing on LSI brand is low-ROI.
7. **Robot OEM Bluetooth diagnostics (DolphinTech Plus)** — real param read / OTA / motor replace. SplashLens has multi-brand *reference*, not live BT. Rating gap (1.7★) still leaves room for better UX *guides*, not replacing Maytronics protocol access.
8. **Remote monitoring hardware (WaterGuru)** — changes service model to exception-based. Ties to Balboa for spas. Adjacent, not competitive unless SL becomes the human escalation packet for alerts.
9. **GPS visit proof / payroll / consumer financing (Paythepoolman GPS; Skimmer Sunbit; PB tech pay)** — company-owner features outside SL lane.
10. **Exploded-view parts diagrams at scale (Heritage 1,000+; POOL360 schematics)** — visual ID without AI. SL PartSnap is complementary if it asks for the diagram proof the counter needs.

**What SplashLens has that almost none of the above have together:** free no-account web pad tools; cross-OEM error encyclopedia; cautious PartSnap proof workflow; spa-pack + multi-robot + automation *reference* in one place; export-first (doesn’t force CRM switch); training-from-misses narrative; Closing Season Mode (AQUA coverage Aug 2026).

## C. White-space / unique wedge ideas

Things **no one clearly owns** that odd, low-patience pool techs would still love:

1. **“Proof Before Counter” layer** — photo → missing-proof checklist → one SMS/WhatsApp packet to boss/SCP counter. Distributors own ordering *after* account login; CRMs own billing; nobody owns the 90 seconds of fitment evidence.
2. **Cross-OEM “weird pad” OS** — Omni + IntelliCenter + iAquaLink + RS-485 symptoms in one non-captive guide (OEM apps are siloed).
3. **Spa-pack rescue for pool-only techs** — Balboa/Gecko/Waterway error + GFCI timing + flow/heater proof. Dealer portals exist; daily-driver field UX for non-spa specialists does not.
4. **Multi-robot field triage** — Dolphin + Polaris + Aiper + Beatbot + WYBOT error/parts without Maytronics login hell. DolphinTech’s poor ratings prove demand ≠ delivery.
5. **Callback insurance packet** — “flow fault + dirty filter + weak model proof = don’t leave” risk flags tied to shareable evidence (SplashLens already teases this).
6. **Closing / opening seasonal proof kits (Midwest)** — drain plugs, cover, declined work, winterize photos → spring dispute killer. Seasonal = natural paid conversion window.
7. **Miss → 5-minute lesson factory** — every PartSnap miss / wrong order becomes apprentice card. PHTA/CPO and mfr universities don’t ingest *your* route’s real fails.
8. **Facility escalation stub (not full CPO LMS)** — QR on wall → readings + photos → packet to contracted service co. WaterGuru automates chem; humans still need incident packets.
9. **Distributor-agnostic SKU handoff** — output Heritage *and* POOL360 search strings / diagram page hints without requiring either account in-app. **[HYPOTHESIS]** affiliate/lead fees possible later.
10. **Voice-first wet-hands notes with structured fields** — Pool Founder has voice for *their* CRM stops; techs on Skimmer/Jobber/paper still need structured proof that pastes out.
11. **“Second opinion” senior-tech async** — photo packet + suggested checks for owners who don’t trust green techs (labor shortage backdrop, Skimmer SoPS 2026).
12. **Warranty-ready evidence packs for OEMs** — standardized fields OEMs wish dealers captured before RMA. Nobody productizes this for pool field.

## G. Recommended 5 bets for next 90 days

Context constraints: **seasonal Midwest**, **fickle software buyers**, **free lookup works**, **PartSnap/camera weak**, **almost no paid subs**, native apps hide Stripe.

### 1. Fix PartSnap → Counter Packet (P0 product) — Effort M  
Ship multi-photo proof ladder + one-tap SMS/WhatsApp/email packet (model, markings, missing proof, search terms). Success = techs *send* packets, not that AI “nails” SKU. Differentiates from every CRM. Directly attacks wrong-part pain Heritage/POOL360 feel.

### 2. Closing Season conversion sprint (P0 GTM) — Effort S  
Lean into AQUA Closing Season Mode coverage: Midwest winterize proof kit, declined-work notes, spring-callback insurance. Offer **annual Pro $249** (or limited winter promo) as “cheaper than one wrong spring reopen.” Don’t sell Teams hard in October—sell **memory of this close**.

### 3. Web Stripe path + “restore Pro” friction kill (P0 monetization) — Effort S  
Native wrappers hide Stripe → almost no paid. Make web checkout → signed activation → restore-by-email dead simple; in-app banners: “Unlock unlimited scans on web.” Measure activation, not App Store IAP fantasies.

### 4. Paste-to-CRM templates + “we’re not Skimmer” campaign (P1 GTM) — Effort S  
One landing + in-app exports formatted for Skimmer / Pool Brain / Paythepoolman / Jobber notes. Skimmer SoPS 2026: all-in-one is standard—**don’t fight the OS, ride it**. Attracts software-fickle owners afraid of rip-and-replace.

### 5. One sponsor path, not five (P1 partner) — Effort M  
Pick **either** (a) Heritage/SCP counter tent-card + deep-link pilot **or** (b) one robot/spa OEM verified-card sponsor (Maytronics handoff *or* Balboa/Gecko spa pack). Do not boil the ocean with Pentair/Hayward enterprise BD in 90 days—their CRM dance partner is already Pool Brain-class.

**Explicit non-bets for 90 days:** AR, live RS-485/BT stacks, building a route CRM, competing with Orenda on LSI brand, Facility/CPO full LMS, App Store IAP.
