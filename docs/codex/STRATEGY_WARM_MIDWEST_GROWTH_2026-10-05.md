## 0. One-paragraph strategy

Run two motions on one product at the same time. **Warm states** (FL, TX, AZ, CA, NV, Gulf/Southeast) are year-round route markets full of **stale, non-tech, and Spanish-dominant crews** who will never "adopt software" but will tap three big buttons, read a code answer in Spanish, and text a proof packet to the boss. **The Midwest/North** is in **closing season right now** (Oct–Nov): winterize checklists, freeze risk, declined-work photos, and spring-dispute insurance turn into a time-boxed, high-intent conversion window. **Pool Brain is the innovative CRM; SplashLens is the proof layer beside it**: paste, export, webhook, never routes or billing. Pricing stays cheap enough to impulse-buy (**Pro $19/mo · $149/yr; Teams owner-paid $49–79/mo with free techs; $5–10 packs**), and the margin thesis is **OEM FOMO** (verified cards, warranty packs, anonymized heatmaps), not seat ARPU. Every PartSnap or search miss feeds a **miss → library factory** that turns field failures into verified cards. That compounds the moat and becomes the asset OEMs pay to sponsor.

## 2. Segment A — Warm-state stale / non-tech / Spanish crews

### Who
- **Stale veterans:** 10–25 years on routes, distrust apps, already "know everything." They still hit unfamiliar spa packs, robots, and connected pads.
- **Non-tech crews:** Low digital patience, gloves on, sun glare, one hand. Won't create accounts.
- **Spanish-dominant crews:** Often the actual hands on the pad at warm-state route companies. Owners are frequently English-first and on Pool Brain / Skimmer / Paythepoolman.

### What they need (product)
1. **Zero login, three buttons.** Counter mode (PC-12): Code / Part / Chem, ≥48px targets.
2. **Spanish that reads like a crew lead wrote it.** Spanish-first toggle (PC-16), auto-suggest from `navigator.language`, Spanish error-code answers, Spanish packet templates.
3. **Text the boss, don't learn an app.** One-tap SMS/WhatsApp proof packet (PC-07, PC-48) with the summary in the tech's language and an optional English line for the owner.
4. **Crew starter link (new, PC-G-16).** The owner texts one link. It opens Counter mode, in the chosen language, with the boss's number prefilled for packets. No account, no seat.
5. **Spa-pack + robot rescue (PC-33, PC-34).** These are the stops where veterans are least confident and where callbacks cost the most.
6. **Fall heater/heat-pump startup content.** **[HYPOTHESIS]** Warm-state heater and heat-pump calls rise as nights cool (Oct–Dec), so heater error codes (MasterTemp, H-Series, AquaCal, Raypak) are a seasonal SEO/AEO wedge there while the Midwest closes.

### How we reach them
- **Owners**, not techs: "Your crew doesn't need another login. Text them this link." The landing is `/crew` (ES/EN).
- **Distributor counters** (SCP/Heritage branches): lunch kit, "snap before you order" tent card, Spanish version.
- **WhatsApp/SMS forwarding:** crews share what works. The packet *is* the viral loop (every shared Passport has a footer link).
- **Spanish search:** "códigos de error Hayward", "identificar piezas de piscina", "app para técnicos de piscina".

### Monetization fit
- Techs stay free (Teams techs-free frame). The **owner pays $49–79/mo** once packets start landing in their inbox (PC-18, PC-19, PC-39).
- Solo Pro $19 for the independent veteran who wants unlimited scans + saved passports.

## 8. Miss → Library factory

The moat is every failed lookup becoming a verified answer.

1. **Capture:** `library_miss` fires on zero-result code search, PartSnap no-match / low tier, or a "didn't help" tap. It carries query, brand guess, equipment family, locale, coarse region, and method. No PII, no photos unless the user opts in to "share this photo to improve the library."
2. **Triage queue (ops):** dedupe by normalized query fingerprint and rank by frequency × recency × callback-risk family. Weekly top-25.
3. **Draft:** create a card draft (problem → what to check → missing proof → when to call OEM) from public manuals/sources with a source link. Mark **Unverified draft** (PC-46 tiers).
4. **Verify:** field-community confirm (PC-49) or partner verification (PC-29). Only server-side flags set "Partner-verified."
5. **Publish everywhere:** in-app card, an SEO/AEO page if it's a public code (with FAQPage JSON-LD), an `llms.txt` entry, a training card (PC-22/45), and Spanish translation queue.
6. **Close the loop:** if the user opted in, "We added the answer you were missing" (`library_miss_resolved`). Heatmap counts feed the OEM pitch.

KPIs: misses/week, median miss→published days (target ≤7), % top-25 resolved, repeat-miss rate on resolved fingerprints.
