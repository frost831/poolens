# PartSnap Positioning And Remaining Gaps - 2026-06-22

## What Was Going On

SplashLens had strong utility, but PartSnap was visually buried as one scanner mode. The app looked like a capable toolbox, not a product with a flagship story. The industry traction is telling us the sharper message is not "another pool app"; it is "the field rescue app that helps identify the mystery part, verify it, and create a clean escalation packet."

## Safer Competitive Claim

Do not claim nobody else has AI part identification. Current market research shows general AI pool tools, Google Lens workflows, and emerging pool-part AI references. Use this positioning instead:

> PartSnap is pool-part identification built into a pool-tech field rescue workflow: possible matches, confidence ladder, verification prompts, Route Brain escalation, and service proof.

## What We Are Still Missing

- A real manufacturer/OEM parts diagram database.
- Vendor-stock/price API integrations.
- Barcode/QR/model-plate OCR tuned specifically for faded equipment labels.
- Human review route for low-confidence part matches.
- Partner verification pages for manufacturers who want an official lane.
- Native iOS speech transcription if Web Speech is unavailable in the wrapper.
- Sponsored banner controls and reporting.
- A public PartSnap landing page with example cases and screenshots.

## Next Best Moves

1. Build a dedicated `/partsnap` marketing page.
2. Add sample PartSnap cases: pump seal, filter grid, salt cell, robot track, valve actuator, light transformer.
3. Create a "submit mystery part" feedback loop for low-confidence scans.
4. Build a parts vendor partner packet for Leslie's, Inyo, SCP/Horizon, Pool360 style distribution, and independent parts sellers.
5. Add model-plate OCR prompts and label-photo capture as a first step before AI part guessing.
