Delete Your SplashLens Account or Data

Submit a verified request here. You do not need to call, send an email, or install the app.

Delete Local App Data

Pool profiles, chemistry logs, route notes, checklists, and service report drafts are stored locally on your device. To delete them, clear SplashLens site/app storage in your browser or uninstall the app/PWA and remove its data.

Request Account or Server Data Deletion

Verify the email used for your SplashLens account, choose a scope, and type DELETE. Requests are processed manually within 30 days, with completion confirmed to your verified email. Submitting a request does not mean deletion has already happened.

A fresh email verification protects your account. No deletion occurs when you send a code.

What Is Deleted or Kept

Account deletion covers your account/profile contact information, user-owned saved proof and content, and identifiable activity associated with the account. A data-only request removes saved content and identifiable activity while retaining the account/profile needed to sign in. Shared user-owned content is included in the review; other team members' accounts are not deleted.

Limited transaction, security, or audit records may need to be retained where legally required. The completion response identifies any retained data, the reason, and the applicable retention period. The request record is kept while processing and its personal data is removed after completion and confirmation. Provider processing and legally required retention may have separate limits described in our privacy policy.

Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a subscription billed by Apple, Google, or another payment provider. You can request deletion now and manage any subscription separately in the provider's subscription settings.
