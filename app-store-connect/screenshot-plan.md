# SplashLens Screenshot Plan

Use 6.7-inch iPhone screenshots first. Add other sizes only if App Store Connect asks for them.

## Required Shots

1. Home/rescue screen with the fastest field action visible.
   Caption: Get off the pad faster

2. PartSnap scan or result with confidence ladder and verification language visible.
   Caption: Identify the possible part path

3. PartSnap escalation packet / missing proof workflow.
   Caption: Verify before ordering

4. Spa or swim-spa reference lane.
   Caption: Pools, spas, and swim spas

5. Closing-season or Service Proof record with photos/readings/notes.
   Caption: Prove the stop

6. Error-code lookup result.
   Caption: Search codes across major pool brands

7. Chemical dosing calculator.
   Caption: Fast pool math in the field

8. Service note/report screen or CRM companion packet.
   Caption: Write a clean service note

9. Optional tester feedback / review prompt after a successful workflow.
   Caption: Help shape the field tool

## Avoid

- Completed certificate claims.
- Official manufacturer status.
- Paid native subscription unlocks.
- Unlimited AI scanning.
- Guaranteed diagnosis, repair, or chemical safety.
