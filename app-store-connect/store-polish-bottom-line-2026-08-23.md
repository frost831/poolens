## Google Play Full Description

SplashLens is a free-core field reference app for pool and spa techs. Use PartSnap to organize possible part paths, search pool and spa code references, calculate common doses, save service proof, capture voice notes, and build cleaner packets for senior techs, vendors, counters, or customer updates.

Use SplashLens for:

- PartSnap photo-assisted part-family clues with missing-proof prompts
- Pool, spa, hot tub, and swim-spa code/reference lookups
- Closing-season and winterizing proof records
- Service Proof records with readings, photos, notes, and customer-safe summaries
- Facility Assist for daily checks, contamination events, and escalation packets
- Chemical dosing math and service checklists
- Voice-friendly field notes

SplashLens is a reference aid. It does not guarantee diagnosis, repair, part fit, chemical safety, warranty approval, code compliance, manufacturer support, CRM sync, or training certification. Always verify against current manuals, model labels, calibrated tests, label directions, and qualified judgment.
