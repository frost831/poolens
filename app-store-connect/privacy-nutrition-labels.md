# SplashLens Privacy Nutrition Labels

Current disclosure posture:

- No account required for core use.
- No third-party advertising tracking.
- No precise location.
- No payment data collected by the initial store wrapper.
- Local service notes may be stored on device/browser storage.
- User-selected images may be uploaded for online AI scanner analysis.
- Product interaction analytics and diagnostics may be collected for app functionality, product analytics, reliability, abuse prevention, and security.
- AI scanner data is for app functionality, not advertising tracking.

Suggested App Store privacy answers for the native wrapper:

- Data used to track you: No.
- Data linked to you: Contact info/account identifiers only when the user creates or verifies a free profile, account, team invite, entitlement, support request, or similar server-backed workflow.
- Data not linked to you: Product Interaction and Diagnostics for app functionality, analytics, reliability, abuse prevention, and security.
- User content: User-selected photos and text may be processed for app functionality if AI/photo mode is enabled.
- Sensitive info: Do not collect health, financial, or precise-location data.

Do not finalize privacy labels until the release build confirms analytics forwarding status, crash reporting SDK status, AI endpoint behavior, image retention, account identity, and subscription identity.
